# ryOS Mobile — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 1, 2026.*

This policy covers **ryOS Mobile**, the native iOS app that runs the ryOS web desktop. The app is provided by Ryo Lu, like the web service. For privacy questions or requests, contact [support@ryo.lu](mailto:support@ryo.lu).

ryOS Mobile is a native shell around the ryOS web desktop. Everything inside the desktop — your account, chats, files, AI features — is handled by the web service and covered by the [ryOS web app privacy policy](/docs/privacy). This page describes only what the iOS app itself and its notification feature add. The app collects no data of its own beyond what is described here: no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

## What the app sends to the notification service

The app itself collects nothing. To deliver chat notifications while the app is closed, it registers with the ryOS notification service — a small feature of the ryOS web service. Registration sends:

- your ryOS username and sign-in state,
- the list of chat rooms you're in,
- an Apple push token that identifies your device for notifications,
- your ryOS sign-in session (so the service can check your chat rooms on your behalf), and
- the app version.

Nothing else leaves the app from the native side. Registration only happens after you sign in to ryOS inside the app and allow notifications.

## How the notification service uses this

The notification service stores, per device: the push token, your username, your chat room list, and a copy of your ryOS session. It uses them for one purpose — checking your chat rooms for new messages and sending them to your phone as push notifications. Message contents are read at the moment of checking and sent to your device as the notification text; the service keeps no messages. It stores a small marker per device and room (the time of the last message it saw) so it does not notify you about the same message twice.

## Third parties

The app shares data with no one except the services named here: the ryOS web service (which includes the notification service) and Apple's push service, which receives the content Apple needs to display the notification. A full list of the web service's providers is in the [ryOS web app privacy policy](/docs/privacy).

## Stopping notifications and deletion

Turning off notifications for ryOS in iOS Settings, or signing out of ryOS in the desktop, stops notifications and deletes the device's registration from the notification service. You can also ask us to remove it at any time by emailing [support@ryo.lu](mailto:support@ryo.lu). Deleting your ryOS account removes your data on the web service as described in the [web privacy policy](/docs/privacy). Deleting the app removes all data the app keeps on the device.

## Consent

The app asks before it sends you any notification, and only registers with the notification service after you sign in. You can withdraw that consent at any time: deny notifications in iOS Settings, or sign out inside the app — signing out also deletes the registration.

## Children

ryOS is not directed to children under the age required for valid consent in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The date above identifies the current version. For questions, email [support@ryo.lu](mailto:support@ryo.lu).
