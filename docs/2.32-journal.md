# Journal

Journal is a local demo of two ways to keep the same days. Pages is the complete journal: a date, a mood score, a streak, a daily reminder, and a weekly report. Freeform is the same text and photos with no scores. Nothing is generated live, and the sample does not need a server.

Open it from Applications, or visit `/journal`. The toolbar switches between Pages and Freeform, and shows Combined while that view is open. The demo drawer holds the feature switches, Reset Demo, and presets for Journal, Freeform, and Two Structures. With dates on, the sidebar lists days through the coming week; empty days are faded. With dates off, the sidebar lists pages and can add an undated page.

Drag a scrap to move it. The position is saved on this device with the rest of the demo state.
