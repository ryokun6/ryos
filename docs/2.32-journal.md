# Journal

Journal is a local demo of two ways to keep the same days. Pages is the complete journal: a date, a mood score, a streak, a daily reminder, and a weekly report. Freeform is the same text and photos with no scores. Nothing is generated live, and the sample does not need a server.

Open it from Applications, or visit `/journal`. The toolbar switches between those two views. The demo drawer, opened from the toolbar, holds the feature switches, Reset Demo, and the Two Structures preset. Two Structures shows both shapes of the same days side by side.

Drag a scrap to move it. The position is saved on this device with the rest of the demo state.
