# ryOS for iPhone — Support

*Last updated: October 1, 2026.*

ryOS for iPhone is the iOS companion to ryOS: the full ryOS web desktop running in a native shell. It loads fast from cache, keeps working offline, notifies you about chat messages while the app is closed, and fills every iPhone display — including the iPhone Duo's large inner display.

## Contact

Questions or problems? Email [support@ryo.lu](mailto:support@ryo.lu) — we usually reply within two business days. The same address is the feedback contact shown in TestFlight.

## Frequently asked questions

### I'm not getting chat notifications.

Notifications need two things: permission granted when ryOS asks, and being signed in to ryOS inside the app. If you answered no to the notification prompt, reinstalling the app gives you the prompt again. When the app is closed, notifications arrive through Apple's push service — allow a minute or two for the first one.

### The desktop loads slowly the first time.

The first launch downloads the desktop from the web. Every launch after that comes from the cache on your phone and starts fast. The download only happens once.

### Does it work with no internet?

Yes. Once the desktop has been loaded once, it starts from the cache on your phone without a network. A banner tells you when the app is offline.

### How do I sign out or delete my account?

Everything about your ryOS account — signing out, deleting your account, exporting your data — lives in the desktop's Control Panels, just like on the web at [os.ryo.lu](https://os.ryo.lu).

### Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Fold it open and the desktop fills the large inner display; fold it closed and it fits the cover display.
